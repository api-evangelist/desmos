We have some important news that we’re excited to share with all of you. The Desmos you know is splitting into two entities: Desmos Studio, a Public Benefit Corporation that develops our free calculators, and Desmos Classroom, the creators of our curriculum and activity platform. The curriculum part of Desmos is then joining forces with Amplify, an education company we’ve been proud to partner with for many years.
